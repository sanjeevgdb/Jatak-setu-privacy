Jatak Setu – Privacy Policy

How to delete your data (Jatak Setu, developer Sanjeev Singla)
1. In the app open Privacy & Terms ▸ My data ▸ Delete my community data. This removes your posts, replies, files, private messages and chat tag from the server.
2. To clear data stored on your phone, use Clear all data on this phone in the same screen, or uninstall the app.
3. Or e-mail sanjeev_gdb@yahoo.co.in and we will delete it within 30 days.
Kept: nothing is kept after deletion, except what Google Play or AdMob hold under their own policies.

Effective: October 2026. App: Jatak Setu (Android). Developer: Sanjeev Singla, India. Contact: sanjeev_gdb@yahoo.co.in, 9316273298.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Jatak Setu works offline. Birth details, charts, client records, notes, settings and branding are stored only on your phone. The developer has no accounts for the app and cannot see this data.

2. Data that can leave your phone

  • Place search (optional). If you search a city online, the place name you type is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Open-Meteo. Downloading a country place pack contacts GeoNames.
  • Advertising (free users, after the free period). Google AdMob shows banner ads and may collect the Advertising ID, IP address and device/app information. Where required (EEA, UK and similar) you are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change it any time in the app (Privacy & Terms ▸ My data ▸ Ad privacy choices). Pro users see no ads. See Google's advertising policy.
  • Purchases. Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment; the developer does not receive card details. The app stores only a purchase record on the phone.
  • Community (optional). If you join the Community you may send text, chart cards (the name or initials and birth data you choose to post), replies, attachments (PDF, image, text), a chat tag and private messages. They are stored in a Google Sheet operated by the developer via Google Apps Script, together with a random member id created on your phone. Public posts are visible to other members. Private replies and private chats are visible only to the people involved and to the developer as administrator.
  • Sharing. PDF, image or WhatsApp sharing happens only when you tap it, through the Android share sheet. The receiving app's own policy applies.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, do not use analytics or tracking SDKs other than AdMob, and do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or device location.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). The Community is for users aged 18 and above.

5. Retention and deletion

On-phone data stays until you remove it in the app (Privacy & Terms ▸ My data ▸ Clear all data on this phone) or uninstall. Community data can be deleted by you at any time (My data ▸ Delete my community data), which removes your posts, replies, files, private messages and chat tag from the server. You can also request deletion by e-mail. Backups are disabled for the app.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), the GDPR and similar laws. E-mail sanjeev_gdb@yahoo.co.in. We reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS. Community access needs a class code. No system is perfectly secure; do not share sensitive personal details.

8. Changes

If this policy changes the date above is updated and the app asks for acceptance again.

9. Disclaimer

Astrology content is for guidance and reflection and is not medical, legal or financial advice.
